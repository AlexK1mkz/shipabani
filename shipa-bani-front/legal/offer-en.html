Services, booking conditions, personal data processing and payment are governed by the applicable legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan. The specific house, date, start and end time, number of guests, service package and price appear in the booking confirmation. Please read these terms before making a payment.

2. Service provider

The service provider is sole proprietor Sergey Valeryevich Ten, trading as Kor Food. The registered address, identification number and contact details appear above. The SHIPÁ venue is located in the Kungey neighbourhood of Karaganda; the confirmed venue address and arrival instructions are provided when booking.

3. Booking and confirmation

Select the available house, date, time and duration, enter accurate guest details and review the displayed total and deposit. Submitting an enquiry without payment is only a provisional request and does not itself confirm a paid booking. An unpaid reservation is generally held for up to six hours after confirmation by the provider, or one hour for same-day reservations, unless other terms are agreed. A booking agreement is concluded when the customer accepts these terms and makes the agreed deposit or when the parties agree another lawful method of confirmation. If payment is received but the house is unavailable due to a technical error, the provider offers an alternative or a refund.

4. Prices, deposits and payment

Prices are shown in Kazakhstani tenge (KZT) and depend on the house, hours, additional guests and chosen services. Unless otherwise specified in individual booking conditions, the standard deposit is KZT 20,000 for Standard houses and KZT 40,000 for VIP houses. The deposit forms part of the service price, rather than an automatic non-refundable penalty. The outstanding balance is due as stated in the booking confirmation. Available payment methods are shown during checkout. Card details are handled by the payment provider when an external payment page is used.

5. Opening hours and visit duration

The ordinary operating hours are 10:00 to 02:00 the next day. Hourly visits may begin between 10:00 and 21:00 when available, with a minimum duration of three hours. The maximum hourly visit is subject to the published rules and confirmed time slot. VIP accommodation can be booked for a full day under a separate rate, generally with check-in at 14:00 and check-out at 12:00 the following day. The confirmed arrival and departure times take precedence. Arriving late does not automatically extend the visit; extensions require availability and agreement.

6. Guest numbers and additional services

The included number of guests is stated for each house. Extra guests may incur a separately disclosed fee, agreed before the service is supplied. Children younger than seven are not counted as paying guests under the ordinary SHIPÁ rules, but accompanying adults remain responsible for their supervision. Additional steam rituals, meals, products or services are charged only where selected and agreed.

7. Cancellation and deposit refunds

Customers may cancel in accordance with Article 686 of the Civil Code of Kazakhstan and applicable consumer rights. SHIPÁ voluntarily offers a full deposit refund for cancellations received at least 72 hours before the booked start time. For later cancellation, deductions must be limited to necessary, actually incurred and documented expenses permitted by law. If no such expenses are proven, the deposit is returned. The provider will provide a reasoned calculation upon request and aims to process an agreed refund within ten business days, preferably to the original payment method. These terms do not restrict statutory consumer rights.

8. Rescheduling

Requests to change the date, time or house category are considered subject to availability and mutual agreement. The deposit is credited to the amended booking. An additional amount is paid only if the new booking costs more and the customer agrees. A reduction in cost is handled by agreement and applicable law. The original booking remains in effect until the provider confirms the replacement slot.

9. Late arrival and no-shows

If the customer arrives late, the booked house remains reserved for the confirmed paid period unless otherwise agreed. A no-show is assessed according to the actual circumstances, applicable civil law and the provider's documented expenses. The deposit is not automatically forfeited merely because the customer did not arrive.

10. Cancellation or non-performance by the provider

If the provider cannot supply the confirmed house for reasons within its control, the customer may accept a suitable alternative or refuse it and receive the payments due under applicable law. Defective services and circumstances beyond either party's control are handled under the Civil Code and consumer-protection legislation of Kazakhstan.

11. House rules and responsibility for property

Guests must follow reasonable health, safety, hygiene and equipment instructions disclosed before the visit, and respect other visitors. Responsibility for damage is determined under Kazakhstan law, taking into account fault and documented loss. Emergencies should be reported immediately and reasonable steps taken to keep everyone safe.

12. Personal data and communications

Booking details and contact information are processed for the stated booking and service purposes under the Privacy Policy and applicable law. Where required, consent is requested separately. Communications by telephone or WhatsApp may be used to confirm practical aspects of the reservation. Marketing messages require an appropriate legal basis or separate consent.

13. Enquiries, complaints and dispute resolution

The customer may contact the service provider by telephone or email with questions, complaints and refund requests. Claims are reviewed under the applicable legislation of Kazakhstan. Where a dispute cannot be resolved amicably, the parties may use the competent authorities and courts under the law.

14. Changes to the offer

The provider may update terms for future bookings. Conditions already agreed for a confirmed booking are not unilaterally changed to the customer's disadvantage. The applicable version should be available before the customer accepts the booking.

15. Final provisions

Matters not addressed by this offer are governed by the legislation of Kazakhstan. The customer should review the currently published terms, confirmed booking details and any individual conditions agreed with the provider.
