1. Consent and the data operator

By actively ticking the separate consent box in SHIPÁ's booking form, I, the data subject identified by the name and contact details supplied in that form, freely and knowingly consent to the collection and processing of my personal data for the purposes specified below. The owner and operator is individual entrepreneur Sergey Valeryevich Ten, trading as Kor Food. This consent is provided in accordance with Articles 7 and 8 of Kazakhstan's Law on Personal Data and Their Protection, No. 94-V.

2. Categories of personal data

The consent covers my name, telephone number, booking and guest details, service requests, payment or refund status and identifiers (but not full card numbers or CVC/CVV), relevant messages, and technical records of the booking and consent process. IIN, identity-document copies, health information and biometric information are not required to provide this consent.

3. Processing purposes

My data may be used to arrange, confirm, change or cancel a booking; contact me about my visit; organise payment, deposits and refunds; deal with service requests or complaints; maintain the website's correct and secure operation; and keep records of consent. This is not consent to advertising, marketing or unrelated third-party promotions.

4. Actions with personal data

I authorise the collection, recording, organisation, storage, updating, use, lawful disclosure, restriction, anonymisation and deletion of my data as necessary for the purposes above. This consent does not by itself authorise decisions that have legal effects solely through automated processing. Processing must be limited to what is necessary, and appropriate protection measures must be applied.

5. Transfer to third parties

Only the data necessary to provide the service may be transferred to technical-support providers, hosting providers, payment organisations and communication providers (including WhatsApp if I choose that channel) for the declared purposes. Data may be disclosed to state authorities where legally required. No permission is given for third parties to use my data for their own unrelated purposes.

6. Cross-border transfers

If I choose WhatsApp, information exchanged through that service may be processed outside Kazakhstan. Cross-border transfers are subject to Article 16 of the relevant Kazakhstan law and do not amount to unlimited permission for foreign data recipients. The operator organises the personal database in Kazakhstan.

7. Publication in public sources

This consent does not authorise public publication of my name, telephone number, visit details, correspondence or other personal data. Separate legal grounds or consent would be necessary before any public disclosure.

8. Validity and retention

Consent takes effect when given and lasts only as long as necessary for the stated purposes. Unconfirmed, unpaid and unfulfilled requests are ordinarily removed from consent-based processing within 30 calendar days of the preliminary reservation ending or the request being rejected. Confirmed booking information may be processed for no more than 12 months after a visit or cancellation, unless the purposes are achieved sooner or a different legal obligation applies. Some payment, tax or accounting records must be kept for statutory periods under a separate legal basis.

9. Withdrawal and rights

I may request information about my data and, where provided by law, correction, restriction, deletion or destruction. I may withdraw my consent by contacting ten_sergey1@mail.ru or the service provider. Under Article 8(7) of the applicable law, the operator responds to a withdrawal request within fifteen business days unless further processing is required by law or another lawful reason applies. Reasonable identity verification may be requested.

10. How consent is confirmed

I give consent through a distinct active action: ticking a box that is not selected in advance after reading this information and the Privacy Policy. The service records the consent, date, time, booking identifier and document version where technically implemented. Consent to personal data processing is separate from agreement to booking terms or marketing.

11. Automated decisions and objections

Routine automatic recording of a booking, availability checks and pricing differ from fully automated decisions that have legal implications. If such decisions are introduced, the operator must disclose their nature and consequences, and obtain any additional consent or other lawful basis required under Article 19-1 of the personal data law. I may object using the operator's contact channels.
