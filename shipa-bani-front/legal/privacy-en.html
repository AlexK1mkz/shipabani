1. General provisions

This policy explains how SHIPÁ, operated by the Kor Food sole proprietorship, handles personal data in connection with enquiries, reservations, payments and customer service. The policy has been prepared with reference to Kazakhstan's Law on Personal Data and Their Protection, dated 21 May 2013, No. 94-V. It is made available before a user submits personal information.

2. Personal data owner and operator

The owner and operator of the personal data is individual entrepreneur Sergey Valeryevich Ten, trading as Kor Food. The contact and registration details appear above. Privacy requests may be submitted using those contacts.

3. Types and sources of personal data

Information may include the customer's name, telephone number, booking date and time, chosen house, number of guests, selected services, booking identifier and status, payment or refund details (excluding full bank-card credentials), correspondence, consent records and necessary technical information such as browser type and IP address. Data is provided by the user or generated while processing an enquiry or booking.

4. Purposes of processing

Data is used to arrange, confirm, change and cancel reservations; contact customers regarding visits; organise deposits, payments and refunds; handle enquiries or complaints; maintain service security; and document consent and booking transactions. The provider must establish a separate lawful basis for any optional advertising or marketing.

5. Legal basis and consent

Where consent is required, SHIPÁ requests an active, informed and freely given action, such as an unchecked checkbox. Consent to processing personal information is not treated as automatic consent to receive advertising or to accept unrelated terms. Users may read the relevant notices before submitting their data.

6. Collection and use of information

Only the information necessary for a stated purpose should be requested and used. The provider does not routinely require an individual's IIN to arrange a bathhouse booking. Publishing reviews, photographs or identifying information requires an appropriate legal basis. If fully automated decisions with legal implications are introduced, additional transparency and legal safeguards are required.

7. Third-party disclosures

Limited personal data may be shared with hosting, technical-support, payment and communication providers only when necessary and lawfully permitted, subject to confidentiality and security measures. WhatsApp messages may be processed under that platform's own terms. Personal information is not sold, and public disclosure is not an ordinary purpose of this service.

8. Storage, retention and international transfers

Personal databases are organised in Kazakhstan in line with legal requirements. Unconfirmed, unpaid and unused enquiries are retained for no longer than needed for the stated purpose; the operator's internal limit is generally 30 days after the preliminary reservation ends. Records of completed bookings may be kept for up to 12 months unless law requires another period. Mandatory accounting and tax records may have separate statutory retention periods. Cross-border data transfers, including through messaging services, are permitted only when the requirements of Kazakhstan law are met.

9. Security measures

The operator takes organisational and technical measures proportionate to risks: restricting staff access, authentication, secure connections, appropriate logging, confidentiality duties and responses to detected incidents. No Internet transfer is risk-free, and customers should not send passwords or full payment-card details through ordinary messages.

10. Your rights and enquiries

Where provided by law, users may request information, correction, restriction, deletion or destruction of their data and withdraw consent. Write to ten_sergey1@mail.ru or use the contact number above. The operator may verify the identity of a requester. Where required by Article 8 of Kazakhstan's personal data law, the operator responds to withdrawal requests within fifteen business days, subject to lawful reasons for continued processing.

11. Cookies and external websites

Cookies necessary for booking functionality and security may be used for their respective purposes. Optional analytics or advertising tools are used only with an appropriate legal basis and controls where required. External websites, payment pages, maps and messaging platforms have their own privacy policies.

12. Children's personal data

The booking service is intended for persons able to enter into the relevant transactions. The provider does not routinely request children's identifying information. Where such information is necessary, it may be processed only on the lawful basis applicable to the child and their representative.

13. Updates and final provisions

The provider may revise this policy when legal requirements, booking processes, technologies or categories of data change. Where a new consent is required, changing the policy does not replace that consent. All other matters are governed by the law of Kazakhstan.
