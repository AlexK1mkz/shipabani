Steam and rituals

Oak, birch or cedar? Choosing a banya whisk

How different branches shape the scent and feel of a steam ritual.

What the whisk does

The attendant uses the branches to move warm air. The rhythm and technique matter as much as the material in the bundle.

Oak and birch

Broad oak leaves can help direct a steady wave of heat. A birch bundle feels lighter and has a familiar leafy scent.

Cedar and conifer branches

Conifers have a more pronounced aroma. Mention any sensitivity to fragrance and ask the attendant what they recommend for your first session.
